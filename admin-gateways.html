1. Cards and wallets

Have the processor approve your exact catalog, seller, settlement bank, currencies and customer countries. Confirm NMI Payment Component and REST v5 support.

Open NMI sandbox signup to prepare provider testing. A sandbox account cannot accept real payments.

Supply the merchant API, tokenization and webhook keys plus merchant ID through protected hosting settings. Keep sandbox credentials and test data in an isolated preview environment. Enable live cards only after account testing.

Apple Pay also needs the account's domain verification file and approved domain. Test wallets and 3-D Secure before enabling their flags.

2. Crypto

Open PayerURL account setup. The account owner chooses and verifies the receiving arrangement, network and fees.

Confirm the account's Node/form API protocol, callback amount unit and checkout hostname. Store its public/secret API pair in protected server settings, then test signed paid, underpaid, canceled and repeated callbacks.

Crypto acceptance does not establish card-acquiring approval. Never enter wallet recovery phrases or private keys here.

3. Interac and bank transfer

In Invoices & transfers, save the real business receiving details: Interac email and exact registered payee, or bank-issued beneficiary, currency and incoming-transfer instructions.

Confirm the account permits the business activity. Verify funds in the bank before recording payment; a customer screenshot is not payment confirmation. Keep fulfillment manual.
