Checkout and payment

Choose products or a nonrenewing membership, sign in, and review the server-calculated total before placing your order. Available payment methods are shown at checkout. Bank transfers receive 5% off merchandise after the better eligible membership or volume discount; shipping and tax are excluded. Send the exact amount and order reference. Payment remains pending until verified bank receipt or authenticated provider confirmation.

Cards and digital wallets appear only when the configured provider account supports them. A bank-transfer checkout is not a card payment. Any card-to-crypto route has a separate disclosure and provider confirmation.

Free account and annual passes

Member: free. Customer account, order/order history, research lists and access to volume pricing.

Alta VIP: US$49 for 365 days; 5% off eligible merchandise at checkout.

Alta Elite: US$99 for 365 days; 10% off eligible merchandise at checkout.

Annual passes are prepaid, nonrenewing purchases. Benefits begin only after AltaPure verifies receipt of the pass payment, then expire after 365 days. No recurring card authorization or automatic renewal is created. Membership discounts do not apply to pass fees, shipping, taxes or other service charges. An active pass cannot be automatically stacked, renewed early or upgraded; contact support for an individually reviewed change.

An unused annual pass may be refunded on request within 14 days of activation. A refund revokes the pass. Requests after a benefit has been used are reviewed individually; nothing here limits applicable statutory rights. Do not buy a pass solely for a single upcoming order: VIP's fee equals its 5% discount on US$980 of otherwise full-price merchandise; Elite's fee equals its 10% discount on US$990. Volume discounts may reduce the incremental value of a pass.

Volume discounts

Order 10–24 units of the same exact product and size for 5% off that line; 25 or more units receive 10% off that line, subject to the reviewed quantity being available. Mixed variants are counted separately. For each line, the better eligible member or volume discount applies; the discounts do not stack. The final order records the actual discount. There is no automatic free shipping or free gift.

Order confirmation and fulfillment

US product checkout charges US$25 standard delivery per order. Typical supplier processing is 1–3 business days, followed by an estimated 3–7 business days in transit. These are estimates, not guarantees. Fulfillment is arranged manually after payment; carrier and tracking are posted to your order. International checkout is unavailable.

The payment window is normally 24 hours and may be shorter when a membership benefit is about to expire. Do not pay an expired or cancelled order. If no payment has been sent, cancel the unpaid order to release reserved balances. If funds have already been sent or a gateway attempt is pending, contact support for reconciliation. We may cancel and refund an order that cannot be fulfilled.

Refunds and payment evidence

Refunds are performed through the original approved bank/provider route where supported and then reconciled to the order. Recording a refund in the admin system does not itself move money. Customer rights and the published refund policy continue to apply. Unrecognized, duplicate, late or mismatched payments require manual reconciliation rather than automatic fulfillment. This launch workflow records full refunds; other adjustments require direct support handling.

Points and store credit

Paid product orders earn 1 point per whole US$1 of merchandise after all discounts and redeemed value. Shipping, taxes and membership fees do not earn points. Each point is worth US$0.01 toward merchandise, with a minimum redemption of 100 points. Issued USD store credit may also pay delivery charges. Neither balance can pay membership fees or be transferred or exchanged for cash. No automatic expiry applies.

Redemptions are reserved when an order is placed. Cancelling an unpaid order restores them. A completed full refund restores redeemed points and credit and reverses points earned on that order. If earned points were already spent, the resulting points debt must be repaid by future earnings before further redemption. Store credit is issued explicitly by staff; a purchase or membership does not invent a credit balance. Historical purchases are not backfilled.

Other programs

No automatic product subscriptions, gift-card purchases, affiliate commissions or partner payouts are created. Research-list cadence is a planning preference, not a recurring order.
