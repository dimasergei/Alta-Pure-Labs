Product pricing and coupon

Products are priced at the catalog unit price for the quantity selected. Enter BLACKFRIDAY15 in your cart or at checkout for 15% off the merchandise subtotal. The code is case-insensitive. Shipping, taxes, optional protection and payment processing charges are excluded. The server-calculated order summary shows the coupon amount before purchase. Currency amounts are calculated and rounded in cents.

Quantity changes and coupon removal update the order total. Prices and product availability are confirmed before an order is placed.

Active PURE WHITE membership provides 5% off eligible merchandise; PURE BLACK provides 10%. Checkout applies the better of the membership discount or an eligible coupon, without combining the two. Any separately available Crypto merchandise incentive applies to the remaining eligible merchandise when a Crypto payment is still due. Membership fees are excluded from merchandise discounts. See the membership terms.

Checkout and payment

Choose your products, sign in, and review the total before placing your order. Available payment methods are shown at checkout. Use the exact amount and order reference in the payment instructions. Payment remains pending until verified receipt or authenticated provider confirmation.

Cards and digital wallets appear only when the configured provider account supports them. Any card-to-crypto route has a separate disclosure and provider confirmation. Any applicable payment processing charge is shown before payment.

Shipping and fulfillment

Standard UPS delivery within the United States is US$14.95. It is free when the merchandise total after any coupon or membership discount is at least US$150, before redeeming existing points or store credit.

Active PURE WHITE and PURE BLACK memberships include standard or express shipping to US addresses. PURE BLACK also includes priority fulfillment. Express delivery without an included membership benefit, next-day service, international shipping and optional shipment protection are separately priced at checkout. Destination availability, carrier coverage, customs and import charges may affect delivery. Included priority fulfillment does not guarantee an arrival date.

Fulfillment is arranged after confirmed payment. The carrier and tracking details are posted to your order after dispatch. Delivery estimates are not guarantees; see the shipping policy.

The payment window is normally 24 hours. Do not pay an expired or cancelled order. If no payment has been sent, cancel an unpaid order to release reserved balances. If funds have already been sent or a payment attempt is pending, contact support for reconciliation. An order that cannot be fulfilled may be cancelled and refunded.

Refunds and payment evidence

Refunds are performed through the original approved bank or provider route where supported and then reconciled to the order. Recording a refund in the admin system does not itself move money. Customer rights and the published refund policy continue to apply. Unrecognized, duplicate, late or mismatched payments require reconciliation before fulfillment.

Points and store credit

Existing issued balances remain available. On eligible paid product orders, active PURE WHITE earns 5 points per US$1 and PURE BLACK earns 10 points per US$1. Earnings use merchandise after discounts and any points or store-credit redemptions; fractional points are rounded down. Points are issued after payment is verified. CAD Interac orders earn points using the underlying USD merchandise value after discounts. A free account does not earn points on new orders.

Each point is worth US$0.01 toward merchandise, with a minimum redemption of 100 points. Issued USD store credit may also pay delivery charges in USD checkout. USD points and store credit cannot be redeemed against a CAD payment or a membership fee. Balances cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash. No automatic expiry applies.

PURE BLACK receives US$50 store credit for a future order after a qualifying paid product order reaches at least US$150 in eligible merchandise after discounts and before points or credit redemptions. This credit is issued at most once per UTC calendar month and does not reduce the qualifying order's total. CAD orders use their underlying USD merchandise value for qualification.

Redemptions are reserved when an order is placed. Cancelling an unpaid order restores them. A completed full refund restores eligible redeemed points and credit according to the recorded order. Product refunds reverse the related earned points and trigger review or reversal of any qualifying monthly credit. Historical earned points are reconciled under the terms recorded with that purchase.
