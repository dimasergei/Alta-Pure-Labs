How launch orders work

Add exact products and sizes to your cart and request an invoice from your verified customer account. AltaPure reviews availability, destination, delivery charges and taxes manually, then issues a final invoice. A request is not an accepted order or a payment. Do not send funds before accepting the issued invoice and checking its beneficiary and payment instructions.

Payment methods are shown only when the business has enabled an actual receiving route. Hosted payment invoices may use an approved card or cryptocurrency provider; this does not mean every payment method is supported. Personal friends-and-family payments are not requested. USD is the catalog reference currency. An issued CAD invoice includes a fixed conversion rate and exact final CAD amount; display conversions elsewhere are estimates.

Free account and annual passes

Member: free. Customer account, order/invoice history, research lists and access to volume pricing.

Alta VIP: US$49 for 365 days; 5% off eligible merchandise on reviewed invoices.

Alta Elite: US$99 for 365 days; 10% off eligible merchandise on reviewed invoices.

Annual passes are prepaid, nonrenewing purchases. Benefits begin only after AltaPure verifies receipt of the pass payment, then expire after 365 days. No recurring card authorization or automatic renewal is created. Membership discounts do not apply to pass fees, shipping, taxes or other service charges. An active pass cannot be automatically stacked, renewed early or upgraded; contact support for an individually reviewed change.

An unused annual pass may be refunded on request within 14 days of activation. A refund revokes the pass. Requests after a benefit has been used are reviewed individually; nothing here limits applicable statutory rights. Do not buy a pass solely for a single upcoming order: VIP's fee equals its 5% discount on US$980 of otherwise full-price merchandise; Elite's fee equals its 10% discount on US$990. Volume discounts may reduce the incremental value of a pass.

Volume discounts

Order 10–24 units of the same exact product and size for 5% off that line; 25 or more units receive 10% off that line, subject to the reviewed quantity being available. Mixed variants are counted separately. For each line, the better eligible member or volume discount applies; the discounts do not stack. The final invoice records the actual discount. There is no automatic free shipping or free gift.

Invoice acceptance and fulfillment

An invoice normally expires 24 hours after issue. Its exact currency, amounts, discount, destination and receiving instructions are fixed for the accepted invoice. Do not pay expired or void invoices. A customer assertion that payment was sent does not mark the order paid. AltaPure checks the business bank/provider transaction before recording receipt. The team then processes fulfillment manually and posts carrier and tracking information in the invoice record.

Supplier automation is not a prerequisite for an accepted manually fulfilled order. However, shipping routes, availability and tax/delivery amounts must be confirmed for that order before payment. No blanket worldwide-delivery or dispatch-time promise is made. Contact support before paying if the invoice does not describe your expected arrangement.

Refunds and payment evidence

Refunds are performed through the original approved bank/provider route where supported and then reconciled to the invoice. Recording a refund in the admin system does not itself move money. Customer rights and the published refund policy continue to apply. Unrecognized, duplicate, late or mismatched payments require manual reconciliation rather than automatic fulfillment. This launch workflow records full refunds; other adjustments require direct support handling.

Programs not offered at launch

No points, cashback, gift-card balance, automatic product subscription, affiliate commission or guaranteed partner payout is earned by a launch purchase. The research builder's cadence is a planning preference, not a recurring order. Partner inquiries do not create a commission agreement. These programs require separately published terms and an implemented accounting workflow before activation.
