Refund Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

At Alta Pure Labs, we are committed to providing high-quality research peptides, compounds and biochemical products. Please read our Refund Policy carefully before placing an order to understand your rights and responsibilities.

01. Purpose of This Policy

This Refund Policy explains the circumstances under which refunds or exchanges are available. All products sold by Alta Pure Labs are strictly for research use only — not for human or veterinary use, diagnosis or treatment. Refunds are provided to ensure customer satisfaction while maintaining the integrity and safety of our products.

02. Eligibility for Refunds

To qualify for a refund or exchange, your order must meet the following criteria:

  • Product must be unused, unopened and in its original packaging.
  • Refund requests must be submitted within 7 calendar days of receipt.
  • You must provide your order number and proof of purchase.

Important: Due to the sensitive and perishable nature of research peptides, we cannot accept returns for opened or partially used products.

03. Non-Refundable Items

The following items are not eligible for a refund:

  • Products that are sold under any sales campaign or through affiliates.
  • Any product that has been opened or used.
  • Products that have exceeded their expiration date or have been mishandled by the buyer.
  • Custom or bulk orders if explicitly marked as non-refundable.

04. Refund Process

To request a refund, please follow these steps:

  • Contact our Customer Support team at altapurelabs@protonmail.com with your order number and reason for the refund.
  • Provide photos of the product and packaging if requested.
  • Our team will review your request and notify you within 3–5 business days regarding approval.
  • If approved, refunds will be issued via the original payment method or as store credit, depending on your preference. Refund processing may take up to 10 business days after approval.

05. Shipping & Returns

Customers are responsible for the cost of return shipping, unless the return is due to a shipping error or damaged product. Products must be securely packaged for return. For temperature-sensitive items, ensure proper handling during shipping to avoid product compromise.

06. Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If you receive a product that is damaged or incorrect, contact us immediately at altapurelabs@protonmail.com. We will arrange a replacement or refund at no additional cost. Please report issues within 48 hours of receiving your order.

07. Orders We Cancel for Verification

Our Research Use Policy lets us confirm that orders are for research use. Under it:

  • We may hold an order and ask for additional information or documents before it ships.
  • If we cancel an order before it ships because we cannot confirm it is for research use, you will receive a full refund to the original payment method.
  • Orders found to be in breach of our Research Use Policy after shipping are not eligible for return or exchange.

08. Legal Disclaimers

All products are for laboratory and research purposes only. Misuse of any product for human or veterinary purposes voids any refund eligibility. Refunds are subject to verification and company discretion, in accordance with applicable laws. Alta Pure Labs reserves the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time.
