Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

At Alta Pure Labs, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you interact with our website and services. By using our website, you consent to the practices described below.

01. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide you with our services efficiently and securely. Types of information we may collect include:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, billing/shipping address.
  • Account Information: Login credentials, order history and account preferences.
  • Financial Information: Payment details (processed securely through trusted providers).
  • Website Usage & Technical Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, device information and website interactions.
  • Cookies & Tracking: Cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze website performance and provide targeted content.

Public-page analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate visits to public catalog and information pages. It does not use advertising cookies. The analytics provider processes technical request information to produce aggregate page, referrer, approximate location and device statistics. Our integration removes query strings and URL fragments, excludes account, invoice, payment and order-tracking pages, and does not send form contents or purchase events. We do not load this analytics script when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

Read Vercel's analytics privacy information. This does not change the essential processing needed for account security, requests or orders.

02. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for:

  • Processing and fulfilling orders, including shipping and payment.
  • Communicating order updates, customer support and account notifications.
  • Improving website functionality and user experience.
  • Sending promotional emails and marketing communications (with opt-out options).

03. Sharing Your Information

We respect your privacy and do not sell your personal data. Information may be shared only in limited situations:

  • Service Providers: Trusted partners for payment processing, shipping and technical support.

  • Third-Party Links: Our website may include links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party websites.

04. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. These include encryption, secure servers and restricted access. However, no system is completely secure. By using our services, you acknowledge the inherent risks of online data transmission.

05. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update or correct your personal information.
  • Request deletion of your information from our records.
  • Opt-out of marketing and promotional communications.
  • Restrict or object to the processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at altapurelabs@protonmail.com.

  • Users are responsible for handling and complying with all applicable laws and safety regulations.

07. Research-Use Verification

To confirm that our products are used for research, we handle the following information:

  • Your research-use declaration: the declaration you accept at checkout, with its date, time, wording version and your IP address, and the purchaser type you select.
  • Proof of affiliation (only if you attach one): a document such as an institutional ID card, a department letter or a business registration. It is sent by email to our verification team through our email provider and reviewed by our staff. We do not save an attached document in our website database or in your order record. We keep it only for as long as needed to complete the review and to meet our legal obligations, and we use it for nothing else.
  • Your order record notes that a declaration was accepted and whether a document was provided.
