Start with the exact compound and strength, then review the available documentation. This guide explains what our supplier references establish and what still needs confirmation for your order.

Explore the catalog ↗Review supplier reports ↗

1. Choose the exact catalog variant

Match the product name, listed strength and quantity. A report for one strength or blend does not automatically cover a different variant. Catalog listings do not by themselves confirm current supplier stock.

Use the research builder to organize your selections or submit a bulk inquiry. These tools prepare a request; they do not establish a paid order.

2. Review the original supplier COA

The COA library links to available original supplier reports. The laboratory, report client, report identifier and lot information remain attributed to the source document.

  1. Check the product identity and strength against the requested variant.
  2. Read the report date, batch or lot identifier, and named client.
  3. Review the tests actually reported and their stated results and limitations.
  4. Ask which supplier lot will fulfill your order and whether its matching report is available.
A supplier reference is not your shipment's lot certificate.

Published reference reports do not establish that AltaPure commissioned the analysis, holds that inventory, or will ship that lot. Missing reports are not replaced with invented certificates. Request the specific supplier and lot documentation before payment when it is required for your procurement decision.

3. Request a reviewed order

Initial fulfillment is coordinated manually. We confirm supplier availability, the destination, shipping service and charge, and applicable order terms before issuing a final invoice. Tracking is added after the supplier dispatches the order.

Payment methods are shown only when available for the reviewed invoice. Cards and digital wallets are not promised until the merchant account and checkout have completed activation and testing. A request or payment-page visit is not confirmation of payment.

Eligible reviewed bank-transfer goods invoices include a 5% merchandise discount, calculated after other line discounts and before shipping and tax. The final invoice shows the discount and an available receiving method. See payment options and the shipping policy.
