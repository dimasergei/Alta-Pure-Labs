Shipping Policy

Effective Date: January 2026

At Alta Pure Labs, we are committed to providing high-quality research peptides, compounds and biochemical products.

01. Order Processing

All orders are carefully reviewed and processed within 1–3 business days. During this time, our team verifies order details, checks product availability and ensures all items meet quality standards.

Please note: Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.

02. Shipping Methods

The current online checkout offers standard shipping to eligible U.S. addresses only. Expedited, temperature-controlled and international shipping methods are not presently selectable at checkout. Product-specific packaging and carrier eligibility must be confirmed before fulfillment.

Tracking information will be provided after a shipment is accepted by the carrier, when available.

03. Shipping Times

For approved U.S. shipments, domestic delivery is generally estimated at 3–7 business days after carrier handoff; this is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date. Actual processing and delivery depend on product availability, fulfillment, the carrier, location and other applicable restrictions.

International delivery estimates are not provided while international checkout remains unavailable.

04. Shipping Costs

The current U.S. online checkout uses a flat shipping charge of $25 USD per order, shown before payment. No free-shipping threshold or international checkout rate is currently configured. Shipping terms and prices may change for future approved services and destinations, subject to being disclosed before payment.

05. Packaging & Handling

Packaging and storage requirements depend on the exact product, lot, fulfillment origin and carrier service. Any necessary temperature controls, labeling, handling and packaging must be confirmed against actual product documentation and the approved shipping route. Insulated or temperature-controlled packaging is not promised for every order.

06. International Shipping & Restrictions

International shipping is not currently available through the online checkout. A country or destination may be enabled only after the exact product, origin, destination, importer/exporter arrangements and shipping service have passed the applicable regulatory and carrier review. A research-use-only label does not independently authorize sale, import, export or shipment. Do not place an order expecting an unlisted destination to be added after payment.

07. Damaged or Lost Shipments

If your order is damaged or lost in transit, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery:

  • Include your order number and photos of damaged packaging (if applicable).
  • We will review the issue and provide replacement or refund options as appropriate.

08. Legal & Safety Disclaimers

All products sold are strictly for research use only. They are not intended for human or veterinary use, diagnosis or treatment. Alta Pure Labs is not responsible for misuse, improper handling or shipping delays outside our control. Shipping policies may be updated periodically; customers are encouraged to check this page for the latest information.
