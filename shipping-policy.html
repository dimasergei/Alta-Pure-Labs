Shipping Policy

Updated November 18, 2026

Free standard shipping — United States and Canada

Standard shipping is free to eligible US and Canadian destinations when merchandise reaches US$150 after applicable merchandise and payment discounts, before points or store credit. The same underlying USD threshold is used for CAD Interac payments, then converted at the checkout exchange rate. Below that threshold, standard shipping is US$14.95 in the United States and US$39.95 in Canada. Other destinations retain their published rates. Optional protection, expedited services, customs and taxes are separate.

Canada and next-day service

Canadian checkout offers standard delivery at US$39.95 below the free-shipping threshold, express at US$59.95 and next-day service at US$69.95. Next-day service is also US$69.95 for US destinations. Next-day refers to the business day after dispatch, subject to destination coverage, order cutoffs, carrier availability and customs clearance. Order processing is separate, and these services do not guarantee delivery the day after an order is placed.

Processing and transit

Orders are arranged manually with a selected supplier after payment is confirmed. Typical processing is 1–3 business days, followed by an estimated 3–7 business days in transit. Weekends, holidays, supplier availability and carrier delays can affect timing. These are estimates, not guaranteed dates.

Carrier and tracking

The available carrier and service are shown at checkout. Tracking is added to your order after dispatch. Carrier coverage may affect express or next-day availability. Temperature-controlled handling must be arranged separately when required.

Availability and packaging

The team confirms supplier availability, handling and packaging requirements before fulfillment. If an order cannot be fulfilled, we will contact you to arrange a change or refund. Original lot-specific documentation can be requested from the team.

Other destinations

International services and rates are shown for your destination at checkout. Import duties, customs and local charges may apply. Contact support@getpurelabs.com if you need help confirming delivery availability or documentation.

Damaged or missing shipments

Contact support@getpurelabs.com with the order reference, tracking and relevant photographs. Please report damage within 48 hours where possible. We will investigate with the supplier and carrier and arrange an appropriate resolution under our refund policy and applicable rights.
