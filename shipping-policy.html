US delivery

Standard delivery costs US$25 per product order. The final charge is shown before you place the order. Online checkout currently supports US delivery only.

Processing and transit

Orders are arranged manually with a selected supplier after payment is confirmed. Typical processing is 1–3 business days, followed by an estimated 3–7 business days in transit. Weekends, holidays, supplier availability and carrier delays can affect timing. These are estimates, not guaranteed dates.

Carrier and tracking

The supplier selects the available standard carrier and service. Tracking is added to your order after dispatch. We do not promise express or temperature-controlled delivery unless specifically arranged for that order.

Availability and packaging

The team confirms supplier availability, handling and packaging requirements before fulfillment. If an order cannot be fulfilled, we will contact you to arrange a change or refund. Original lot-specific documentation can be requested from the team.

Other destinations

International checkout is unavailable. Contact altapurelabs@protonmail.com before ordering for another destination; adding a country to an address does not establish delivery availability.

Damaged or missing shipments

Contact altapurelabs@protonmail.com with the order reference, tracking and relevant photographs. Please report damage within 48 hours where possible. We will investigate with the supplier and carrier and arrange an appropriate resolution under our refund policy and applicable rights.
